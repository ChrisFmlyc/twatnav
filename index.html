01 How it works

  1. 1

    Drop a pin

    Set your radius — this street, this city, this festival. Go invisible with one tap whenever you fancy it.

  2. 2

    Say what you're after

    A date, a mate, a pool team, a co-parent, a nothing-serious Tuesday. Tag it up front so nobody's guessing.

  3. 3

    Actually meet

    Nearby-first means plans that happen. Local queer nights, walking groups and meets, right in the grid.

02 Who it's for

Lesbians. Bi and pan women. Trans women — full stop, no asterisk. Non-binary and genderqueer sapphics. Ace and aro folks who want closeness on their own terms. Late bloomers. People who worked it out at fifteen and people who worked it out last Tuesday.

Exclusionary politics, racism, fatphobia and transphobia get you removed. That's the whole policy, and it isn't up for debate in the comments.

03 Built safe, not sorry

Fuzzy location

Distance, never coordinates. Your exact position never leaves your device, and snap-to-grid stops anyone triangulating you.

Verified humans

Optional photo verification with a visible badge, so you know she's her before you leave the house.

Real moderation

Reports read by actual queer people, not a keyword filter. Block is permanent and silent.

Discreet mode

Not out yet? Hide your profile from contacts, blur photos until you unlock them, and use a neutral app icon.
